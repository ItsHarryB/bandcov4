Throughout March with versions 26.3.x, there have been a number of changes:

* **v26.3.0** involved updating configs.

* **v26.3.1** involved optimisations, fixes (such as images not loading)

* **v26.3.2** involved key optimisations - such as converting the (then) javascript social component to an astro component

* **v26.3.3** involved uninstalling react, installing preact, as well as making further changes and optimisations. As well, view transitions were implemented, as well as making other small visual tweaks.

* **v26.3.4** involved changing the location of the "My CV" page and implementing further optimisations.

* **v26.3.5** involved implementing a content security policy for the site, as well as making changes to the appearance of the blog page and blog posts.

* **v26.3.6** involved fixing dark theme issues, adjusting page animations and fixing photo carousel behaviours.
