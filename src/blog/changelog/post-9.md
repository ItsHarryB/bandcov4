With the launch of v4.2.0, there have been significant background changes to the functioning of the Brighton and Co website.

These significant background changes include:
- Removing react
- Installing preact
- Removing social.jsx for social.astro.

This is all with the aim of improving the performance and efficiency of the Brighton and Co website.

For v4.2.x, further improvements have been made to the effiency of the website. As well, visual improvements have been made, with the implementation of view transitions.
